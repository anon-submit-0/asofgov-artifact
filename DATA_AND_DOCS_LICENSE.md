## Upstream datasets (NOT redistributed here)

This repository intentionally ships **no** BIRD or Spider source data and no
`warehouse.duckdb` built from them; `fetch_and_rebuild.sh` downloads from the
official channels and rebuilds locally.

* **BIRD** (dev set, `dev_20240627`) — <https://bird-bench.github.io/> —
  distributed by its authors under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. If you redistribute
  rebuilt warehouses or any content derived from BIRD data values, you must
  comply with CC BY-SA 4.0 (attribution + share-alike) and cite:
  > Li et al. *Can LLM Already Serve as a Database Interface? A BIg Bench for
  > Large-Scale Database Grounded Text-to-SQLs.* NeurIPS 2023.
* **Spider** (dev, `world_1` database) — <https://yale-lily.github.io/spider> —
  distributed by its authors under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Same obligations; cite:
  > Yu et al. *Spider: A Large-Scale Human-Labeled Dataset for Complex and
  > Cross-Domain Semantic Parsing and Text-to-SQL Task.* EMNLP 2018.

The per-domain `questions.json` / `gov_seed/` files contain small numbers of
values computed from BIRD/Spider rows (gold answers, coverage hulls); they are
kept here because the evaluation is unusable without them, and they remain
subject to the upstream CC BY-SA terms to the extent they embed upstream
values.

## Frozen LLM responses

`pilot2/runs/<arm>/*.json` are cached responses generated by the commercial /
open-weight models named in `pilot2/PREREG_pilot2_arms.md` (claude-opus-4-6,
qwen3-coder-next, deepseek-3.2, minimax-m2.5). They are provided for exact
reproducibility of the paper's scoring; check the respective providers' terms
before any other reuse.
